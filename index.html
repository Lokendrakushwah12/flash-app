FlashEdit

The Meitu-style flash look for your Mac. Darker, moodier backgrounds, a flash-lit person, real skin tones. Rendered on your Mac, offline, the same every time.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Free · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon & Intel

BeforeAfter

Drag to compare. Illustration; FlashEdit works on your own photos.

Everything for the flash look

Start from a look, then fine-tune any part of it, or just tell Claude what you want.

⚡︎

Four looks + Strength

Looks learned from real reference edits, a None option, and a Strength slider to fade any edit in or out.

◐

Isolate

Edit the whole photo, only the background or only the person. Apple Vision finds them for you.

✦

Skin & lips

Face and body tone, plastic shine from glossy to matte, and pink lips traced from the lip outline.

◑

Light, curves & colour

Exposure to blacks with Auto, a tone curve you draw, HSL for eight colours and colour-grading wheels. Search any slider with ⌘F.

◎

Blur like a lens

Round bokeh, gaussian, motion, zoom or tilt-shift behind the person, who stays sharp.

⌗

Crop, detail & effects

Aspect ratios and straighten, sharpening and noise reduction, vignette, grain and fog.

◍

Heal & masks

Click a blemish to heal it from a matched nearby patch. Brush, linear and radial masks, each with its own light and colour.

✳︎

Chat with Claude

“Teal and orange, blur the background.” Claude moves the sliders, never the pixels. Pick Fable, Opus, Sonnet or Haiku.

▣

A library that remembers

Every photo keeps its edit, chat and versions. Virtual copies, copy and paste edits, star ratings, undo with ⌘Z.

⌖

Zoom & compare

Before and after side by side. Pinch to zoom, hold Space to pan, hold to see the original.

▶︎

Video too

A timeline to trim, split and reorder clips, hide or mute layers and record a voice-over. Edit on any frame, save frames as photos, and export with the look on every frame.

⇪

HEIC in, HEIC out

Drag photos from Finder or straight from Photos. Export at full resolution as JPEG or HEIC.

Private by design

Your photos never leave your Mac. Person and face detection run on-device with Apple Vision, and the edit is plain maths on your CPU, so the same settings always give the same picture. Chat is optional and only talks to the Claude Code app you already have installed.

Install in a minute

FlashEdit isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks once before the first launch.

Download

Get FlashEdit.zip from the latest release and double-click it.

Move to Applications

Drag FlashEdit.app into your Applications folder and open it.

Allow it once

If macOS can't verify it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FlashEdit.app
